Murder Week (Project MW)

Seven nights this town will never forget.

Role(s)

Programming Lead, Gameplay Programmer, Character Storyteller

Tools Used

Godot - GDScript

Team Size

9 Developers

Production Period

August 2026 - Present

Overview

Murder Week is a game where the player can play as one of seven killers, each representing one of each of the seven deadly sins, whos goal is to eliminate as many civilians as possible before getting caught and apprehended by police officers. Throughout the week, the player can choose which killer they'd like to play as as the game grows in difficulty as more rounds progresses. The game ends when the player is apprehended. To further enhance the difficulty, the player can toggle on modifiers ranging from increasing the number of required civilians to eliminate or the damage of police officers.

My Contributions

  • Currently writing character bios and lore regarding their motivations to become serial killers
  • Leading 4 active programmers across several paradigms, involving UI/UX, gameplay, and graphics programming

Technical Highlights

  • An inheritance-driven character system, allowing polymorphism and abstraction to exist in character interactions with the world
  • A NPC life cycle currently involving being fed information when spawned while having their reference stay intact for future pooling.
  • A reactive health bar attached to most characters' inherited scenes that show how much damage was taken given a particular time frame that appears only when the character is damaged

Project Outcome(s)

  • Promising progress involving character interaction, life cycles, and behavior in the current early stage of development

DIE ROBONINJA DIE

Hide in trash cans and slash people open!

Role(s)

Gameplay Programmer

Tools Used

Unity, C#

Team Size

3 Developers

Production Period

July 11th, 2026 - July 19th, 2026

Overview

In this game, you play as a ninja who was abducted by members of a bio testing agency to have experients ran on you, and to get your revenge, you eliminate endless waves of their strongest guards.

My Contributions

  • Programmed behaviors of the enemies, ranging from movement to attacking to death
  • Shared ideas on design of the level to ensure fairness for the player
  • Programmed key navigation methods so each enemy the player could be accounted for

Technical Highlights

  • Implemented a custom enemy pathfinding system in using a depth-first search algorithm over a connected platform graph, finding reachable routes to the players platform
  • Developed enemy behaviors for pursuit, wandering, and hiding-spot investigation, integrating navigation with combat and environmental interactions.
  • Created a platform-detection system, allowing for level designers to easily place two types of Prefabs (walking and jumping-dropping platforms) to move around
      • Created a character pooling system, allowing enemies who have been eliminated to essentially respawn with the same default behavior

Project Outcome(s)

  • Created my first full-scaled game in Unity using C#
  • Created a reusable pooling algorithm and platform navigation system
  • Placed 37/54 overall in the associated GameJam based on ratings from the community and other developers
